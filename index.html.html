About

I'm a final-year BS Biotechnology student and published researcher driven by a fascination with how molecular biology, genetic engineering, and bioinformatics can solve real-world problems in health and the environment. My work spans hands-on molecular diagnostics, computational genomics, and the green synthesis of nanomaterials — and I care deeply about making science accessible to others.

Research & Projects

Published · Planta Animalia, Vol. 5

Detection of Candida Species in Stored Apples

Co-authored peer-reviewed research confirming Candida species across diverse regions of Punjab, using PCR amplification with ITS1/ITS4 primers and molecular species confirmation.

ORIC-Funded FYP · 1 of 6 selected

Green-Synthesized Silica Nanoparticles

Green synthesis of silica nanoparticles from Cuscuta reflexa extract, evaluated for antimicrobial and antilarval activity with in-vivo toxicological assessment for biomedical safety.

In-Silico Genomics

Resistance-Gene Mapping in A. baumannii

Mapped chromosomal integration and genetic regulatory switches of antibiotic-resistance genes using BPROM and ResFinder.

Book Chapter · In Progress

Medicinal Plants for Nanoparticle Synthesis

Lead contributor on African-origin medicinal plants for nanoparticle synthesis, exploring antimicrobial and antilarval in-vitro biological activities.
